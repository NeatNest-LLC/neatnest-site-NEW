Carpet & Upholstery Cleaning FAQs

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

Drying time varies by carpet type, airflow, and humidity, but most carpets are dry within a few hours. We recommend keeping the area well-ventilated—open windows and run fans if possible—for the fastest drying. Avoid heavy foot traffic until the carpet is fully dry.

Do you clean area rugs and upholstered furniture?

Yes. We provide carpet and upholstery cleaning services, including many area rugs and common upholstered furniture materials such as sofas, chairs, and ottomans. If you're unsure whether we can clean a specific fabric or rug type, contact us for guidance before your appointment.

Will carpet and upholstery cleaning remove pet odors and stains?

Many pet stains and odors can be significantly reduced or removed depending on how long they've been set and the type of material. We apply targeted pre-treatment for pet stains and use deodorizing solutions designed for fabric surfaces. We'll recommend the best approach after a quick assessment of your carpets and furniture.

How should I prepare before a carpet cleaning appointment?

Before we arrive, please remove small items and breakables from the areas to be cleaned. Clearing the floor of clutter, toys, and small furniture helps us work more efficiently. We can discuss furniture moving options during scheduling so we're aligned on what will be moved.

How often should I have my carpets professionally cleaned?

Most homes benefit from professional carpet cleaning every 6–12 months. Homes with pets, young children, or heavy foot traffic may want more frequent cleanings—every 3–6 months—to maintain freshness and extend the life of the carpet. We're happy to help you decide on the right schedule for your household.
